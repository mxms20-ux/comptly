Sanctorum

Product Design · UX Research · Responsive Web Experience

A contemporary digital space for discovering the lives of Catholic saints through their stories, virtues, and moments of reflection.

Case Study in Progress

1. Project Strategy

Project Context

Many digital resources about Catholic saints present information in traditional, text-heavy formats. Sanctorum explores an alternative approach: a visually engaging, accessible experience designed around the interests and everyday lives of younger audiences.

The project is being developed as an independent product design concept, beginning with discovery, user research, and early visual explorations.

Project Framework

Status: In Progress
Role: Product Designer
Platform: Responsive Web Experience
Primary Audience: Young People
Initial Research Focus: Ages 15–24
Scope: UX Research, Competitive Analysis, Information Architecture, Brand Identity, UI Design, Interactive Prototyping, and Usability Validation.

Problem & Opportunity

Problem Existing information about Catholic saints can be difficult for young audiences to engage with when presented through dense text and conventional digital experiences.
Opportunity Explore a contemporary digital experience that combines religious content, visual storytelling, and accessible navigation to encourage discovery and reflection.
Design Question How might we help young people discover and connect with Catholic saints through an engaging, relatable, reflective, and easy-to-explore digital experience?

Product Concept

Sanctorum is envisioned as a digital space where young people can explore saints not only through their biographies, but also through their choices, struggles, experiences, and spiritual teachings.

The experience aims to encourage curiosity first, followed by opportunities for personal reflection, prayer, and continued exploration.

Early concepts include a saint of the day, discovery through virtues, short saint stories, optional prayers, and a personal reflection journal. These are ideas under exploration, not confirmed product features.

Experience Principles

Approachable Present stories in clear, manageable formats that invite exploration.
Relatable Connect saints' experiences with themes meaningful to young people's lives.
Engaging Use visual storytelling and intuitive navigation to encourage curiosity.
Respectful & Faithful Historical and religious fidelity takes priority over emotional engagement. A saint's story should never be altered to fit an emotional narrative.
Serene & Meaningful Create space for reflection while maintaining a contemporary, clear, and visually appealing experience.

2. Research & Insights

Research Focus

The discovery phase explores how young people encounter stories about Catholic saints, what motivates them to engage with religious content, and which digital formats could make the experience more relevant and accessible.

Initial research considers different levels of familiarity with saints, religious participation, personal interests, and digital habits.

Research Questions

Discovery How do young people currently discover saints and religious stories?
Comprehension What information helps users understand a saint's life and significance?
Engagement Which content formats and visual elements encourage exploration?
Trust What makes religious information feel credible and worth revisiting?

User Survey 6 Exploratory Responses

An exploratory survey was conducted through Google Forms to understand participants' digital habits, familiarity with Catholic saints, content preferences, and expectations for a potential digital experience.

The six responses provide preliminary signals for further investigation. They do not represent the broader target audience.

Early Survey Signals

Participant Age All six respondents selected the 25-or-older age group. The initial target audience of 15–24 remains untested in this sample.
Trust 5 of 6 respondents selected reliable information as an important reason to continue exploring a website.
Visual Content 4 of 6 respondents selected short videos as an attention-grabbing format, while 3 of 6 selected images and illustrations.
Search 4 of 6 respondents selected a saint search feature among their preferred website functions.
Personal Relevance 3 of 6 respondents expressed curiosity about the challenges saints faced, and 3 of 6 selected what they could learn from their stories.
Content Barriers 3 of 6 respondents selected difficult-to-understand information as a potential reason for losing interest.

These results describe only the six survey participants. Additional research is needed before making broader audience or product decisions.
